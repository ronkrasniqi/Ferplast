Ferplast 🇽🇰

Ferplast is a Kosovo-based plastic manufacturing company founded in 1996 and located in Ferizaj, Kosovo.

We specialize in manufacturing high-quality plastic products and piping solutions for water supply, drainage, electrical infrastructure, construction, and other industrial applications.

🏭 About Ferplast

Ferplast operates on a 200,000 m² site and has more than 20 production lines. Since its establishment, the company has expanded across Kosovo and exports products to countries in the Balkan and European markets.

📦 Products

Our product range includes:
	•	Corrugated drainage and sewer pipes
	•	HDPE water-supply pipes
	•	Electrical cable protection pipes
	•	Drainage pipes
	•	PP-H and PP-H Silent pipes
	•	Optical-fiber cable pipes
	•	HDPE water reservoirs
	•	Plastic inspection chambers and related products

🧪 Quality

Ferplast focuses on quality-controlled manufacturing and produces products according to relevant European and international standards, including standards such as EN, ISO, and DIN, depending on the product.

🌍 Our Mission

Our mission is to provide reliable, durable, and high-quality plastic solutions while supporting infrastructure development in Kosovo and international markets.
